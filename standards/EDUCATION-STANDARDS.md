# Interwoven Education Standards

**Part of the central governance in [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md)**. Applies to `Interwoven-Education` hub and any `Interwoven-[Subject]-Education` repositories.

## Core Requirements
- **Free Core Principle**: All pathways, labs, tutorials, templates, submissions, and reference materials must remain **permanently free**. Paid official classes, cohorts, or certifications are allowed only as extensions that clearly link to the free repository materials.
- **Attribution**: Follow [ATTRIBUTION.md](../ATTRIBUTION.md). Any paid course must credit all contributors listed for the underlying content.
- **Structure**:
  - Use templates from this Community repo.
  - Labs follow pedagogical patterns (Bloom’s taxonomy, project-based artifacts, reflections) per existing `Interwoven-Education/ROADMAP.md`.
  - Clear citation to Libraries: "See Interwoven-Security-Library/handbooks/..." 
  - Student submissions in `submissions/` or linked.
- **Review Gates**: Normal PRs for content. Teaching/official class approval is a **separate formal process** (see GOVERNANCE.md "Teaching Approval Request" issue). Even major contributors to a course must submit the request for official listing/scheduling.
- **Non-contributors**: Admins/Moderators (including Squirrel814) can approve experienced educators who did not directly contribute to the material, provided they meet criteria and commit to the free-core + attribution rules.

**Monetization**: Paid classes are permitted if core materials stay free and attribution is maintained. Approval ensures quality and community alignment.

See full details in [GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md), including the exact commitment language, qualification criteria, and public draft status.

*Maintained centrally in Interwoven-Projects-Community. Feedback via Issues/PRs encouraged.*
